The timestamps appear to be recording artefacts from an audio/video source used during digitisation (running clock or kirtan timecode). They are spread across Angs 361–536 only. The colon characters and digits are ASCII and therefore invalid in the `text` field per the allowed-character spec.

**Full list of affected IDs:**
16542, 16596, 16654, 16708, 16747, 16790, 16837, 16871, 16905, 16951, 17005, 17063, 17107, 17162, 17210, 17266, 17314, 17362, 17396, 17432, 17467, 17502, 17538, 17582, 17634, 17687, 17742, 17794, 17848, 18019, 18230, 18696, 18753, 19055, 19285, 19413, 23493, 23809

**Recommendation:** Strip the timestamp tokens from `text` (regex `\d{1,2}:\d{2}:\d{2}`) and optionally move them to a separate `audio_offset` field for provenance.

### Analysis of patterns

| Sihari + full vowel (ਾੀੁੂੇੈੋੌ) | 0 | Clean |
| Sihari at word-end | 9,508 | **Normal** — locative/instrumental case marker in Gurbani grammar (ਦਰਸਨਿ, ਮਨਿ, ਮੁਕਤਿ) |
| **Sihari + Virama (੍)** | **2** | **❌ ENCODING ERROR** |

The only genuine misplacements are the two known cases:

### Case 1 — id=25118, Ang 573

```
text:     ਏਕ ਦ੍ਰਿਿ੍ਸਟ ਹਰਿ ਏਕੋ ਜਾਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਆਤਮ ਰਾਮੁ ਪਛਾਣੀ
gurmukhi: ਏਕ ਦ੍ਰਿਿ੍ਸਟ ਹਰਿ ਏਕੋ ਜਾਤਾ ਹਰਿ ਆਤਮ ਰਾਮੁ ਪਛਾਣੀ ॥
translit: ek drisat har eko jaataa har aatam raam pachhaanee
```

Problem word `ਦ੍ਰਿਿ੍ਸਟ` codepoint sequence:
```
U+0A26 ਦ  DA
U+0A4D ੍  VIRAMA
U+0A30 ਰ  RA
U+0A3F ਿ  VOWEL SIGN I   ← sihari #1
U+0A3F ਿ  VOWEL SIGN I   ← sihari #2 (DUPLICATE — ERROR)
U+0A4D ੍  VIRAMA         ← virama after sihari (WRONG ORDER)
U+0A38 ਸ  SA
U+0A1F ਟ  TTA
```

**Likely intended word: ਦ੍ਰਿਸਟਿ** (drisṭi — sight/divine vision)
Correct sequence: ਦ + ੍ + ਰ + ਿ + ਸ + ਟ + ਿ

### Case 2 — id=25593, Ang 586

```
text:     ਸੋ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਧਨੁ ਧੰਨੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਉਪਦੇਸੁ ਦੇ ਸਭ ਸ੍ਰਿਿ੍ਸਟ ਸਵਾਰੀ
gurmukhi: ਸੋ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਪੂਰਾ ਧਨੁ ਧੰਨੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਹਰਿ ਉਪਦੇਸੁ ਦੇ ਸਭ ਸ੍ਰਿਿ੍ਸਟ ਸਵਾਰੀ ॥
translit: so satigur pooraa dhan dhann hai jin har upades de sabh srisat savaaree
```

Problem word `ਸ੍ਰਿਿ੍ਸਟ` codepoint sequence:
```
U+0A38 ਸ  SA
U+0A4D ੍  VIRAMA
U+0A30 ਰ  RA
U+0A3F ਿ  VOWEL SIGN I   ← sihari #1
U+0A3F ਿ  VOWEL SIGN I   ← sihari #2 (DUPLICATE — ERROR)
U+0A4D ੍  VIRAMA         ← virama after sihari (WRONG ORDER)
U+0A38 ਸ  SA
U+0A1F ਟ  TTA
```

**Likely intended word: ਸ੍ਰਿਸਟਿ** (srisṭi — creation/universe)
Correct sequence: ਸ + ੍ + ਰ + ਿ + ਸ + ਟ + ਿ

Both errors share the same pattern: a duplicate sihari followed by virama+consonant, apparently caused by a copy-paste or OCR mis-sequence during digitisation. The transliterations (`drisat`, `srisat`) are correct for the *intended* word, confirming the encoder's intent. The `gurmukhi` field has the same error, so the fix must be applied to both `text` and `gurmukhi`.

## THREE MOST SERIOUS ISSUES

### Issue 1 — CRITICAL: 38 Rows with Embedded Timestamps in `text`
**Check 2 | Angs 361–536**
ASCII time-strings like `18:42:58`, `20:30:47`, `28:27:38` appear mid-line in the `text` field, corrupting 38 Gurbani lines. This is the highest-priority fix: a simple regex strip will resolve all 38. The anomaly is concentrated in an early digitisation batch (Angs 361–536) and does not affect later Angs, suggesting a source-change mid-corpus.

### Issue 2 — HIGH: Sihari Encoding Errors at ids 25118 (Ang 573) and 25593 (Ang 586)
**Check 3**
Both words contain a duplicate sihari (ਿਿ) followed by virama (੍), corrupting the conjunct consonants ਦ੍ਰਿਸਟਿ and ਸ੍ਰਿਸਟਿ. The error affects both `text` and `gurmukhi` fields. Must be corrected at the codepoint level: remove the duplicate U+0A3F and the misplaced U+0A4D.

### Issue 3 — LOW: Raag `ਵਡਹੰਸੁ` / `ਵਡਹੰਸ` Spelling Split
**Check 5 | Angs 557–594**
The same raag (Vadhans) is encoded as two distinct strings: `ਵਡਹੰਸੁ` (Angs 557–585, 1,044 lines) and `ਵਡਹੰਸ` (Angs 585–594, 366 lines). Any raag-based grouping or search will silently miss ~26% of Vadhans lines. Normalise to one canonical form.
