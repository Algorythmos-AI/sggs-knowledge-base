# Code of conduct

This project builds a study application over Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji for a community that trusts
it with its scripture. Everyone who takes part — maintainers, contributors, interns, students,
reviewers — agrees to the following.

1. **Reverence for the text.** The Gurmukhi text is treated as sacred and kept verbatim. Nobody edits,
   normalises, "corrects", paraphrases or jokes about it. Concerns go to a scholar through a
   scripture-fidelity issue, never into a change. The full practice is the wiki's
   [reverence checklist](docs/onboarding/reverence-checklist.md).
2. **Respect for people.** We welcome contributors of every faith and none, every background and
   every level of experience. We disagree about code, never about the worth of a person. Harassment,
   discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.
3. **Honesty in engineering.** We report what we verified, say what we did not, and do not present
   a guess as a fact — about the text, about the code, or about a deploy.
4. **Care with what people entrust to us.** The apps collect no personal data and the logs never
   carry what people search. We keep it that way, and we never paste secrets anywhere.

Questions or concerns about conduct: **support@gurbanisoul.com**. The maintainers respond to every
message and act on every credible report; a serious or repeated breach ends a person's participation.
