Widgets and Live Activity

22 September 2026 widgets

Gurbani Soul reaches a little way out of the app, onto your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so the parts you return to are a glance away. Everything here runs on the device, from the same bundled data as the rest of the app.

Home Screen widgets

The widgets show the parts of the day you check most: the current watch of the Raag Clock, the last Hukam you drew, and today’s Nitnem with your progress. They update quietly in place and never ask for a connection.

The reading Live Activity

While you are reading a longer bani you can start an optional Live Activity — a live indicator on the Lock Screen and, on supported devices, the Dynamic Island. It shows that a reading session is in progress and lets you return to your place.

By deliberate design, the Live Activity never shows a line of scripture. The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island are shared, glanceable surfaces, and it would not be fitting for Gurbani to appear there out of context. The Live Activity carries only the fact of a session and a way back into the app, where the verbatim text is read in full.

Siri and Shortcuts

Gurbani Soul offers a small set of Shortcuts actions and Siri phrases so you can, for example, draw a Hukam or open the app to a place without tapping through. These are ordinary system features you opt into; they run on the device and add nothing that watches you.

There is a principle underneath all of it: the further a surface is from your full attention, the less scripture belongs on it. A widget can carry the shape of your day — the watch, your Nitnem progress, that a Hukam was drawn — while the Hukam’s words themselves wait for the moment you open the app and give them your attention. Nothing here notifies you unless you ask it to, and nothing runs in the background collecting anything.

All of this is optional. If you would rather just open the app and read, nothing here gets in the way. The complete Granth is always a tap away in the reader, and the same verified text is on the web in the Knowledge Base.
