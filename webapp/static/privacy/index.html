Privacy Policy

We collect nothing. Here is exactly what that means.

Last updated 16 September 2026. Applies to the SGGS app for iPhone and iPad (bundle org.sggs.app, including its Home Screen widgets) and to this website.

The short version

  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.
  • The app makes no network connections. It works fully in Airplane Mode.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs — in the app or on this site.
  • Everything you do in the app (saved verses, settings, reading position) stays on your device.
  • Location is optional, used only on your device, and never transmitted.

The app

The app bundles the complete text of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji and searches it locally. It never sends a request to any server, so we cannot see what you read, search for or save. Specifically:

  • Saved verses are stored in the app's own database on your device. If you save a verse, it is also added to your device's Spotlight index so you can find it from the Home Screen search; unsaving removes it. Spotlight data stays on your device.
  • Settings (accent colour, transliteration, traditional rendering, last Ang read) are stored in the app's local preferences.
  • Widgets read a small snapshot (the current raag watch and the last Hukam verse) that the app writes into its own App Group container on your device. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Location is used only by the Raag Clock's optional solar mode to compute local sunrise and sunset. It is requested only when you turn that mode on. The fix is rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) before it is stored in the app's local preferences, and the raw position is never kept. You can enter a location manually instead, or deny the permission; the clock still works.
  • Integrity checks read the bundled scripture database's size and modification date to decide whether its checksum needs re-verifying. This is why the app's privacy manifest declares the "file timestamp" and "user defaults" API reasons. Nothing is displayed or transmitted.
  • Diagnostics: if the app ever crashes or hangs, iOS may hand it a diagnostic report. The app writes that report to its own local folder so a maintainer can inspect it with your help; it is never uploaded. Separately, Apple may share crash reports with us through App Store Connect only if you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in your iOS Privacy & Security → Analytics settings. Those reports are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no account or identity information.

Because the app collects no data, its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

This website

  • The site is static and contains no analytics or advertising scripts, and sets no cookies.
  • Your browser's local storage holds your theme choice, the traditional-rendering toggle, and any verses you pin to the Study Trail. This lives in your browser only; clearing site data removes it.
  • Searches and page requests are answered by our API. The hosting providers we use (Vercel for the site, Render for the API) keep standard server logs — IP address, user agent, request path and time — for security and operations, under their own retention rules. We do not add tracking to those logs, do not build profiles from them, and do not share them.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your data, your control

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device: deleting the app removes it. Everything the site stores is in your browser: clearing site data removes it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new text is published here before it takes effect. The app will not start collecting data without a new version, a new policy, and a new App Store privacy label.
