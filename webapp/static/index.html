01 Read

The whole Granth, set to be read

All 1,430 Angs in Sant Lipi, on warm paper or in a night scheme that is never pure black. Turn pages, jump to any Ang, pick up where you left off. Every composition opens with its printed heading.

  • Sant Lipi
  • Traditional saroop
  • Five accents
  • Night scheme
  • iPad landscape

02 Search

Search the way you remember

A word in Gurmukhi, a word as it sounds in Roman letters, or just the first letters of each word — the way a line comes back to you. Auto mode works out which you meant. Verify checks a line you were sent against the text and tells you plainly.

  • Auto
  • Gurmukhi
  • Roman
  • First letters
  • Theme
  • Verify

Made for every day, not for scrolling.

No feed. No notifications you did not ask for. The text, and your place in it.

03 Nitnem

Nitnem, with your place kept

The daily banis by time of day — Japji, Jaap, Savaiye, Chaupai and Anand in the morning, Rehras at dusk, Sohila at night — with Sukhmani, Asa di Vaar and Ardaas alongside. The app remembers where you are in each and keeps a quiet record of the days you read. Reminders are optional, local, and yours to set.

Banis from Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji come from the same verified corpus, cited by Ang. Dasam Granth and Ardaas text are a separate, labelled, scholar-reviewed layer.

04 The watch

Eight watches. Thirty-one raags. One face.

The day is divided into eight pahars, and different raags belong to different watches. The Raag Clock shows which belong to this one — by fixed hours, or by your local sunrise and sunset. The deep night is left in silence.

See the Raag Clock

05 At a glance

On your Home Screen and Lock Screen

Three widgets — the raag of the moment, a Hukam verse with its Ang, today's Nitnem — in sizes from small to large, plus Lock Screen accessories. An optional Live Activity shows your reading progress as a ring. It never shows a verse.

Siri: “Today’s Hukam”, “What raag is it now”. Shortcuts: “Open Ang”. Saved verses appear in Spotlight.

06 Explore

Explore the Granth from every side

Themes across the whole text. Lineage — 30 voices, 12th to 17th century. Insights that count and compare. A constellation of verses that share their words.

07 Verbatim

Nothing corrected. Nothing invented.

The text is reproduced character for character from the source edition. Nothing is normalised, modernised or improved.

Never AI-generated.

  1. 1

    Reconciled

    All 1,643,385 characters are proven against the source edition, character for character — zero loss.

  2. 2

    Sealed

    The bundled text carries a SHA-256 checksum, fixed when the app is built.

  3. 3

    Checked

    The app checks it when it is installed or updated. If a check fails, the app refuses to show the text rather than risk showing it altered.

08 Private

Nothing leaves your device.

No account. No network connections — the app works in Airplane Mode. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party code. Its App Store privacy label is ‘Data Not Collected’.
