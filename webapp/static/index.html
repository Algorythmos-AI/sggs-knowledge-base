Everything, on your device

Made for reading, not for scrolling

The whole Granth, the daily banis and the study tools — quiet, exact, and entirely on your device. No feeds, no accounts, nothing asking for your attention.

Read the whole Granth
All 1,430 Angs in a clear Gurmukhi typeface. Turn pages, jump to any Ang, resume where you left off. Every composition opens with its printed heading.
Search how you remember
By word, by how it sounds in Roman letters, by first letters the way Gurbani is recalled, or by theme across the whole text.
Nitnem, with gentle reminders
The daily banis by time of day, your place kept in each, and a quiet record of the days you read. Optional reminders you set yourself.
Hukam & the Raag Clock
Draw a complete Hukam any time. See which raags belong to the current watch of the day, with an optional solar mode.
Widgets & Live Activity
The current watch, the last Hukam and today's Nitnem on your Home Screen; an optional reading Live Activity that never shows a verse.

The Raag Clock

The raags of this watch

The dial follows the eight pahars — the watches of the day.

  • ਦੇਵਗੰਧਾਰੀ devagandhaareeਬੈਰਾੜੀ bairaarheeਬਿਲਾਵਲੁ bilaaval
  • ਗੂਜਰੀ goojareeਟੋਡੀ todeeਸੂਹੀ sooheeਬਿਲਾਵਲੁ bilaavalਗੋਂਡ gondਸਾਰੰਗ saarang
  • ਵਡਹੰਸੁ vadahansਧਨਾਸਰੀ dhanaasareeਮਾਰੂ maaroo
  • ਮਾਝ maajhਗਉੜੀ gaurheeਤਿਲੰਗ tilangਤੁਖਾਰੀ tukhaaree
  • ਸਿਰੀਰਾਗੁ sireeraagਜੈਤਸਰੀ jaitasareeਮਾਲੀ ਗਉੜਾ maalee gaurhaaਕੇਦਾਰਾ kedaaraaਬਸੰਤੁ basantਕਲਿਆਨੁ kaliaan
  • ਬਿਹਾਗੜਾ bihaagarhaaਸੋਰਠਿ sorathਨਟ natਮਲਾਰ malaarਕਾਨੜਾ kaanarhaaਜੈਜਾਵੰਤੀ jaijaavantee
  • The deep night is left in silence.
  • ਆਸਾ aasaaਰਾਮਕਲੀ raamakaleeਭੈਰਉ bhairauਪ੍ਰਭਾਤੀ prabhaatee

Times follow your device's clock; the app can also follow sunrise and sunset.
