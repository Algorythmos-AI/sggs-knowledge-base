The promise

Sacred text deserves exactness.

The Gurmukhi in Gurbani Soul is reproduced character for character from the source edition, and the app verifies it every time it launches. Nothing is corrected, normalised, paraphrased, or generated.

AI helps you find and navigate — it never writes Gurbani. When you search by word, by sound, or by first letters, you are always shown the exact verse, with its Ang. If a line isn't in the Granth, the app tells you so, plainly.

A reader, a search engine, and a daily companion — with no network connection ever made.

Faithful by construction

How you can trust it.

Concern about AI distorting Gurbani is well founded. Gurbani Soul is built to be the opposite of that.

✅

Verified at every launch

The bundled scripture is checked against a certified checksum (SHA-256) each time the app opens. If it ever failed, the app would refuse to show scripture rather than risk showing altered text.

🅰️

Always cited by Ang

Every verse carries its Ang. Explanation, when offered, is clearly labelled as explanation — never blended into the original.

👳

Reviewed by people

If anything in the Gurmukhi ever looks wrong, it is checked against the printed Bir by a person before anything changes. We never "correct" the text ourselves.
