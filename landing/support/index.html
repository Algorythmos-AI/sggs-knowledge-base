Help with Gurbani Soul

Gurbani Soul is a fully offline reader and search tool for Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, all 1,430 Angs. This page answers the common questions; anything else, write to us.

Contact

Email hello@gurbanisoul.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and the app version shown in More → About. We aim to reply within a few days.

If something in the Gurmukhi looks wrong, tell us the Ang and the line, and a photo of the printed page if you have one. We never "correct" the text ourselves: the app reproduces the source edition character for character, so every report is checked against the printed Bir by a person before anything changes.

Frequently asked

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The whole Granth is bundled inside the app. It never connects to any server, so it works on a flight, in a basement, or with mobile data switched off.

Is the AI writing Gurbani?

No. AI in Gurbani Soul only helps you search and navigate. Every verse shown is the exact, verified scripture with its Ang — nothing is paraphrased, normalised, or generated. If a line isn't in the Granth, the app says so.

Why is there no English translation in the app?

The English translation we would like to include is licensed for personal study only, and distributing it through the App Store requires a written licence we do not yet hold. Rather than ship it without permission, the app is Gurmukhi-only, with transliteration. If a licence is granted, the translation will arrive in an update as a separate, clearly labelled layer — never mixed into the scripture.

What does the "traditional saroop" toggle do?

It changes only how the text is drawn: the doubled subjoined ya is shown as the single tucked form used in the printed Bir. The stored text, search, and Copy are always the verbatim characters. Turn it off in the reader's options to see the exact source characters.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only if you switch the Raag Clock to solar mode, which times the watches of the day by local sunrise and sunset. The location is rounded to about 1 km, kept only on your device, and never transmitted. You can deny the permission and enter a location manually. See the Privacy Policy.

The widgets are empty.

Open the app once and open a Hukam; the widgets read a small snapshot the app writes. If they stay empty, check More → About → Integrity and confirm it says the Widget App Group is available, then email us with that screen.

How do I save, share, or find a verse later?

Long-press a verse (or use the actions on a verse card) to Save, Copy, Share or Explore related verses. Saved verses appear under More → Saved and in your device's Spotlight search. Share cards always carry the Ang citation.

Siri and Shortcuts

The app offers "Today's Hukam", "What raag is it now", "Search Gurbani" and "Open Ang" as App Intents. Links of the form sggs://ang/500 or sggs://hukam open the app directly.

Accessibility

The app supports VoiceOver (each verse is read as one item, in Punjabi, with its Ang), Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes, Reduce Motion, Bold Text and Increase Contrast. If something is unreadable or unreachable with assistive technology, that is a bug: please tell us.

About the text

The Gurmukhi is reproduced verbatim from the source edition and cited by Ang throughout. Transliteration is a mechanical reading aid, not the scripture. The Gurmukhi typeface is Sant Lipi, © Shabad OS, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
