# Gurbani Soul — Brand Book

Status: **adopted** — G1 passed 2026-09-18; phase 2 shipped to `integration` (PRs #28–#31). G3 and G4 remain open before App Store submission. Scope: the iOS app, its widgets, icon and share card.
The Knowledge Base website is a separate scholarly property and keeps its own name and theme.


Companion files: [`tokens.json`](tokens.json) (source of truth) · [`contrast-report.md`](contrast-report.md)
(generated by `scripts/brand/contrast_report.py`; every ratio quoted here comes from it).

---

## 1. Brand idea

**Gurbani Soul is a quiet, exact companion to Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji.** Verbatim, cited by Ang, never invented.

| Layer | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Product | **Gurbani Soul** | What people download and open |
| Endorser | **Built by Algorythmos** | Credit line only (About, store listing) |
| The text | **Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji · Ang N** | Never branded, never abbreviated, never styled as a product |

Personality: reverent · exact · warm · unhurried · plain-spoken.

Colour story: **gold leads** — the gold of Harmandir Sahib and of basanti — on warm paper and warm ink.
**Red is a rare accent.** Gurmukhi is always set in ink, never in a brand colour.

## 2. Where the palette comes from, and what we leave behind

The palette values are taken from a McDonald's brand kit held locally as reference (`Brand-files/`, git-ignored).

**We take:** the hex values; an 8-pt spacing grid; radii 4 / 8 / 12; short spring motion; *one primary action
per screen*; *red under ~10% of any screen*; *no gold or red body text*; sentence case; flat before gradient;
a pre-ship checklist.

**We leave, without exception:** the Golden Arches in any form; the "Token" (a gold mark on a red rounded
square); cropped-arch graphics; watermark patterns; the Speedee typeface; price badges; food-photography
rules; the playful retail voice and tagline.

> **Hard rule — no gold mark on a red square, anywhere** (icon, avatar, launch, widget, marketing).
> Individual colours are free to use; that *combination* is another company's trade dress.
> Professional clearance (gate G4) is required before App Store submission.

The kit's printed contrast ratios are inaccurate (it states 11.2:1 and 1.9:1 where the real figures are
9.94:1 and 1.69:1). This book quotes only computed values.

## 3. Colour

### 3.1 The one rule that makes gold work

Brand gold `#FFBC0D` is **1.69:1 on white and 1.58:1 on reader paper**. It cannot be text, an icon, a link
or an unbordered shape in light mode. So the accent is two tokens:

| Token | Use | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| `accentFill` | The literal brand gold. Prominent fills only: primary button, selected pill, hero card, widget rule | Always carries a 1-pt `accent` border in light mode |
| `accent` | App tint, SF Symbols, links, toggles, borders, focus | >= 3:1 on card and paper |
| `accentText` | Accent-coloured words on a surface | >= 4.5:1 on canvas, card, paper |
| `onAccent` | Label sitting on `accentFill` | >= 4.5:1 on the fill |

In dark mode all three gold tokens are the same `#FFBC0D` (10–11:1 on warm ink) — gold is at its best there.

### 3.2 "Soul Gold" — the default palette

| Token | Light | Dark | Light, Increase Contrast | Dark, Increase Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `accentFill` | `#FFBC0D` | `#FFBC0D` | `#8A6100` | `#FFC72C` |
| `accent` | `#A87900` | `#FFBC0D` | `#8A6100` | `#FFC72C` |
| `accentText` | `#8A6100` | `#FFBC0D` | `#5C4100` | `#FFC72C` |
| `onAccent` | `#2B1A05` | `#2B1A05` | `#FFFFFF` | `#2B1A05` |
| `accentDeep` (hero second stop) | `#FFB81C` | `#C08B00` | `#6E4E00` | `#D9A200` |

Worst-case measured ratios: `accentText` 4.96 (light canvas); `accent` 3.64 (light paper);
`onAccent` 5.54 (light Increase Contrast). Full table in the contrast report.

### 3.3 Supporting brand colours

| Colour | Light | Dark | Role | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand red | `#DA291C` | `#DA291C` | **Fill only**: badge, count, one-off emphasis; white label 4.87:1 | Never text (4.36:1 on canvas). Under ~10% of a screen. Never behind or beside Gurmukhi |
| Maroon | `#8A1538` | `#E0658A` | Optional categorical / special | Passes as text on all legs |
| Kraft | `#B69A81` | `#B69A81` | Dividers, paper tint, illustration | Never text (2.48–2.65:1) |

The status reds, greens and blues stay exactly as they are (`Ink.positive / negative / info / special`).
`Ink.negative` `#B33528` remains the only *text* red.

### 3.4 Surfaces — unchanged

Light: system grouped backgrounds (`#F2F2F7` canvas, `#FFFFFF` card) and reader paper `#FBF7F0`.
Dark: the warm ink ramp `#151210` / `#1E1A17` / `#282320`, paper `#171412`. Never pure black, never the
kit's neutral greys — gold needs warmth under it, and this ramp is already regression-tested.

### 3.5 The other accents stay

Saffron, Gold (antique, `#B07D12`), Indigo and Teal remain in the accent picker with their current values
and labels. Anyone who already chose one keeps it; only readers who never chose move to Soul Gold.

### 3.6 Categorical colour (Lineage, Insights, Clock, echo bands)

Meaning is never carried by colour alone — pair with a label or symbol. With gold as the lead, contributor
kinds and chart series must stay clear of `#FFBC0D` and `#DA291C`; keep the existing info-blue / special-violet /
teal set, and move "Guru" from saffron to `accent` so it follows the palette.

## 4. Typography

| Text | Face | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Gurmukhi | **Sant Lipi** (bundled, OFL) | Untouched. Ink colour only. Reader size setting and Dynamic Type preserved |
| Screen titles, section headings, hero lines | **Warm serif, headings only** — candidates Fraunces, Source Serif 4 (both OFL) | Always `relativeTo:` a text style so it scales to AX5; never below 17 pt; never for buttons, tabs, lists |
| Everything else | San Francisco (system) | System text styles, no fixed point sizes |

Choosing the serif (gate G1), in order: sits calmly beside Sant Lipi; clear lining numerals for Ang numbers;
holds up at AX5 without truncation; font file size; OFL text shipped next to the font as with Sant Lipi.
Recommendation going in: **Source Serif 4** (quieter, more bookish); Fraunces is the expressive alternative.

Style: sentence case everywhere. No all-caps. Emphasis by weight, not by colour.

## 5. The mark and the app icon

The mark is **ੴ set in Sant Lipi** — flat, single colour, never outlined, shadowed, tilted or gradient-filled.
Clear space: half the mark's height on every side. Minimum size 16 pt.
Permitted colours: brand gold, warm ink `#171412`, paper `#FBF7F0`, white.

| Concept | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | Gold ੴ on warm ink `#171412` | Distinctive on a home screen; furthest from any food-brand look; dark and tinted variants are trivial |
| B | Ink ੴ on brand gold | Strongest colour ownership; closer to retail yellow |
| C | Gold ੴ on paper | Softest; weakest contrast at small sizes |

1024 x 1024, flat, not pre-rounded; iOS 18 dark and tinted variants supplied. Red is not used in the icon.

## 7. Voice

Reverent, plain, exact. Short sentences. Say what a thing is. Never playful about the text, never promotional
inside the reader, never "AI" as a selling point. Every quotation carries its Ang. Explanation is always
labelled as explanation. UI strings that tests depend on are frozen unless the test suite is updated with them.

## 8. Accessibility

All text pairs >= 4.5:1 and all UI pairs >= 3:1 across light, dark and both Increase Contrast modes — proven
by the contrast report and, once implemented, by `ThemeContrastTests`. Tap targets >= 44 pt. Dynamic Type to
AX5. Reduce Motion honoured through the existing motion helpers. Gold, red and maroon are never the sole
signal of state.

## 9. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Let gold lead; one gold action per screen | Set words, icons or links in `#FFBC0D` on a light surface |
| Border every gold fill in light mode | Put a gold mark on a red square |
| Keep Gurmukhi in ink on paper | Colour, tint or decorate scripture text |
| Use red for a badge, sparingly | Use red for text, large areas, or near Gurbani |
| Use the serif for headings only | Set buttons, lists or body in the serif |
| Cite "Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji · Ang N" | Call the text "Gurbani Soul" |
